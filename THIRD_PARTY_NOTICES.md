Exercise naming and baseline movement classification were informed by [Free Exercise DB](https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db), released into the public domain under The Unlicense. The catalog in this project is a small, independently reviewed and extended subset with original safety, substitution, timing, and planning metadata.
